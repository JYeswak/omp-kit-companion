**Config files and registries hold only irreducible human decisions. Any fact a probe can read live (versions, holds, leases, in-use state, paths, counts) is derived at run time and never copied into a file. A hand-copied fact goes stale and later needs a scrub.**

A file may hold a decision (a cap, a hold, a preference, an approval) with who and when; a fact is read live by a named probe. Record a decision as `decision: ...; reason: ...; who: ...; when: YYYY-MM-DD` on the literal line or in a complete immediately preceding `# decision:` comment. Otherwise use the runtime probe instead. The decision record remains the human authority; this rule refuses only the unreasoned derived literal.
