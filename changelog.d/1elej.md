### Added
- `omp-kit verify BEAD --export SHA --self-replay` lets a bead's assignee replay their own acceptance boxes on the exact published export before handing over (SELFREPLAY1). Until now `verify` ran its close preflight before every replay, `--dry-run` included, so an author was refused `NON_AUTHOR_REQUIRED` (or `LESSON_MISSING`, or `NOT_IN_REVIEW` for in-progress work) and had no way to see which box would fail; the action plan told authors to dry-run verify before handover anyway, and the first-attempt rate stayed at 36 against a target of 90. The new mode runs the same export, publication check and box judgement, prints each box's verdict and output as overall `SELF_REPLAY`, exits 1 when a box fails, and closes nothing, ticks nothing, writes no tracker act, needs no lesson and allocates no verification attempt, so it never counts as the non-author verification. Anyone but the assignee is refused `AUTHOR_REQUIRED`, and the flag does not combine with `--dry-run`, `--live-readback` or a background verifier. Plain `verify` by the author is still refused.
