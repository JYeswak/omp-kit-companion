### Fixed

- The pre-push gate no longer leaks its archive extracts into `var/agent-tmp`: its last step ran under `exec`, which replaced the shell holding the cleanup trap, so every push left one or two full source extracts behind (196 dirs, 6.1 GiB measured on 2026-10-12). The gate now runs that step as a child and exits with its status, so cleanup runs on green and red (ompkit-fp72.40).
