- A handoff to `in_review` (`br update --status in_review --transition-comment 'DONE <sha>'`) now admits a cited commit that exists in the cited repository even when it is not yet published on `origin/main`, so a builder can hand unpushed work to their director, who publishes. Closing with the same citation is still refused `NOT_PUBLISHED` until the commit is published, and citations that resolve nowhere are still refused on both paths.
