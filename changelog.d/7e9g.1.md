### Fixed

- Scratch reclaim now runs before the disk alarms instead of after them (RECLAIM1). A pressure tick classifies free space as `WARN` at 25% or less and `RECLAIM` at 20% or less. At `RECLAIM` it starts the existing `scratch-reaper` job (`launchctl kickstart` on macOS, `systemctl --user start` on Linux), whose own lock serializes every apply. A manual `omp-kit scratch apply` returns BUSY (exit 4), naming the holder PID, while the scheduled reaper holds that lock. The quarantine TTL is configurable as `quarantine_ttl_hours` in `scratch-reaper.json` (1 to 168, default 24); an invalid value fails `service run scratch-reaper` (exit 1) instead of being ignored. Reaper runs report free space and scratch usage per root. [test]
