- A native send whose own turn fails to compile (`carly ok:false` with `syntax error: lua#N:...`) no longer holds the seat's pending record for the acknowledgement window: it now clears the pending attempt, writes the recovery copy, and reports definite `NOT_DELIVERED` (`SEND_REFUSED_NATIVE`), so the next wake sends at once instead of being suppressed `PENDING_CONSUME`. Every other native failure keeps the pending record as before.
