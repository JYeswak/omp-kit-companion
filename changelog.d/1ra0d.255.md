### Fixed
- The scheduled scratch reaper no longer spends its 30 minute cap measuring directories it will not touch. A scratch dir under an ovnfs checkout's `var/agent-tmp` is checkout-owned: the reaper skips it, counts it per verdict but never as reclaimable bytes, and acts on nothing in it. Its verdict still walked every such dir to fill a size nobody read: on 2026-11-21 that was 11,458 dirs (351 GB of logical bytes across 20 NFS-served checkouts) of a 13,651-row census, and the read-only plan took 690 s before a single reap (the run is killed at 1800 s: `TIMEOUT`, exit 1, since 2026-11-12). The checkout-owned verdict now carries `sizeBytes: 0` ("bytes not measured", the count is unchanged); the same plan on the same machine takes 240 s with identical reapable (34.3 GB) and quarantinable (21.1 GB) totals. `omp-kit checkouts plan` reports a checkout's bytes on demand.
