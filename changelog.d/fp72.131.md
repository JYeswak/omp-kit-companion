### Added

- `kit-flywheel-guard` refuses `br update --status blocked` on a bead with no open blocks edge to a non-closed bead, unless the bead carries the `human-gate` label backed by a `HUMAN-GATE: <effect only Joshua can perform>` comment. A closed blocker, a parent-child edge or a related edge is not a fix; an unreadable bead is refused. This stops blocked beads from piling up with no fix (BLOCK_REFUSED).
