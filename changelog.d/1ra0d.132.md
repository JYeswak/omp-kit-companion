- The stale-worktree commit check now tells a revert apart from an ordinary edit per hunk: a hunk that re-adds a peer commit's removed lines still needs a `Revert:`/`Supersedes:` trailer no matter what else it adds, while any other removal of peer lines passes with an `Authored-On: <base>` trailer when that base already contains the peer commit. A stale base, an unknown base, or no trailer refuses exactly as before, and the refusal names the ready trailer block for both ways out. `omp-kit land --patch` accepts `--base SHA` to prove the patch against that tree and record the trailer automatically.
