- A kit runner killed mid-run no longer leaves its child sessions running. Every process that calls `pysafe.run_bounded` (and the TypeScript `kitlib run`) now starts one parent-death reaper (`pysafe.py reaper`, in its own session) and registers each child session with it over a pipe. When the caller dies, even by SIGKILL or by SIGTERM without `run_main`, the pipe closes and the reaper kills every registered session whose leader still has its recorded start time; an unrelated session is never touched. Timeouts, cancellation and leader exit now sweep the child's whole session by `os.getsid`, so a descendant that moved to its own process group (`setpgid`) no longer survives the call. This is the 2026-11-04 CFS publish, where git's pre-push hook and node outlived a killed `git_converge.py` for about 50 minutes.
