- `omp-kit send` refuses a work dispatch (first token `D`, or a line starting `queue:`) that cites no bead id, before anything reaches tmux: error code `BEAD_REQUIRED` (kernel inv 4, beads are the execution substrate). Bead ids are whole tokens shaped `<prefix>-<seg>[-<seg>…][.<n>…]`; URLs, paths, the D-line slug and `queue:` slugs do not count. Results carry `bead_ids` and `no_bead_reason`; `--no-bead-reason "<why>"` sends a deliberate exception and records it. DONE/BLOCKED returns, `R:` replies and notes are unaffected.
