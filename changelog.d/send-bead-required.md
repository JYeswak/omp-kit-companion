- `omp-kit send` refuses a work dispatch (first token `D`, or a line starting `queue:`) that cites no bead id, before anything reaches tmux: status `BEAD_REQUIRED` (kernel inv 4, beads are the execution substrate). Bead ids are whole tokens shaped `<prefix>-<seg>[-<seg>…][.<n>…]`; URLs, paths, the D-line slug and `queue:` slugs do not count. Results carry `bead_ids` and `no_bead_reason`; an explicit exception passes via `noBeadReason` (CLI `--no-bead-reason` follows). DONE/BLOCKED returns, `R:` replies and notes are unaffected.
