### Changed

- `omp-kit verify BEAD --export SHA --self-replay` now leaves a receipt under the kit state dir (bead, export sha, a digest of the box letters, phases and bodies, actor, time, and each box's verdict and output digest), pass or fail, and exits non-zero when the receipt cannot be written. Ticking boxes afterwards does not invalidate it; editing a box does.
- The flywheel guard refuses `br update BEAD --status in_review` unless the assignee's self-replay receipt for the bead's current boxes shows every [build]/[landed] box PASS; the refusal prints the exact `omp-kit verify BEAD --export SHA --self-replay` command (with `--tracker REPO` when the tracker is elsewhere). [live]/[after]/[v] boxes and beads without boxes are exempt, and the guard never runs a replay.
- The same gate refuses an unresolved or missing bead id, an unreadable bead (retry), an acceptance edit in the same `br update` (edit acceptance, self-replay, then move), and a `DONE repo@sha` line whose sha is not the receipt's export or whose ref is not a sha (other hashes in the comment are ignored); an admitted move notes the receipt's export sha, and refusals print that sha and `--tracker REPO` when the tracker is elsewhere.
