- Self-restart no longer strands an active goal as paused: a new `kit-goal-state` seat extension publishes the live goal state on every `goal_updated` event, so when omp 18.18.8 leaves a stale `goal_paused` in the journal while the goal is actually live-active, the helper trusts the newer same-goal record and resumes it once after the restart, while a person-paused goal still stays paused.
- A directed paused continuation that journals nothing new now counts as a preserved pause instead of failing the restart: on omp 18.18.8, reattaching an already-paused goal journals no new pause record.
- The goal-state record is registered in the kit's ownership registry, so its shutdown and crash cleanup is checked like every other per-seat record.
