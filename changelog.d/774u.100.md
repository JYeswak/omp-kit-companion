- A wake steered to a busy Tern seat is no longer recorded failed when its entry lands just after the short proof poll: proof of an admitted steer now waits up to the caller's until-delivered bound (capped by the proof deadline, 20 s by default), so the marker journaled at the seat's next turn boundary is proven delivered and leaves no pending record to suppress the following wakes. Observation and steer admission still never wait on a busy seat.
