# Flywheel invariants at the act: kit-flywheel-guard

The `package.json` OMP manifest declares `extensions/kit-flywheel-guard.ts` (ompkit-xa5s). `COMPACTION_REREAD`: after OMP's `session_compact` event the guard injects a reminder naming the AGENTS.md nearest the session cwd and telling the agent to `br show` its in-progress bead before continuing (appended to context when idle, injected at the next step boundary during a run). `SELF_CLOSE_REFUSED`: a bash `br close <id>` or `bd close <id>` is blocked when the bead's assignee, read with `br show <id> --json` in the call's cwd, is the caller (`--actor`/`--agent-name`, `BR_ACTOR`, `BR_AGENT_NAME`, `AGENT_NAME`, or the Agent Mail pane identity file for `$TMUX_PANE`); the reason tells it to report DONE with evidence and leave the close to the director or a verifier. Unknown identity, an unassigned bead, or a failed `br show` allow the close with a warning.
