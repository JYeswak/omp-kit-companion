### Fixed
- The complete-suite gate waits for its owned native Bun system-shell supervisor before publishing a cancelled or timed-out terminal. The phase worker, carrier manifest and process helpers participate in judging identity; incomplete runs remain non-reusable, and suite coverage and deadlines are unchanged.
- `pysafe.run_bounded` gives a cancelled or failing-output-consumer carrier its existing TERM grace before final KILL/reap, allowing that carrier to drain separately grouped descendants. A failing output callback is not re-entered during cleanup. Plain process-group cleanup does not claim ownership of escaped processes.
